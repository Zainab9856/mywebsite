A Dash of Biology

"Flower" or "blossom(s)" is the term used for the reproductive part of flowering plants.
Now, I want you to look at the two flowers shown below.

Beautiful as they are, it's a wonder they share the same function when they look so different. But why is that?

Well, it largely comes down to evolution. The Earth is vast and plays host to many different environments. Ergo, the specific pollinators of each environment are different, and flowers have had to adapt to them.
Take the Himalayan Blue Poppy (Meconopsis betonicifolia), which has papery, saucer-shaped petals that prevent the flower from snapping when harsh mountain winds blow. In contrast, the Angel's Trumpet (Brugmansia) is funnel-shaped, as it has adapted to pollinators like hawkmoths, drawn in by its amplified smell.
